# Stage 4: Scenario-aware ranking

Generated by `scripts/track2/s4_rank.py` from `config/track2/candidates.json`.
Tables: `tables/s4_ranking.tsv` and `tables/s4_stress.tsv`.

## How the scores are computed

- **Rubric** (out of 100): direct BUB1B/MVA evidence 25; mechanistic fit to
  the scenario 20; downstream phenotype evidence 15; paediatric and
  regulatory suitability 15; oncology and kidney compatibility 15;
  feasibility 10.
- **Evidence items:** c1 and c3 are lists of items, each with a source, so
  any single source can be removed and the effect measured.
- **Scenario weighting:** the expected score weights each scenario by the
  Stage 1 priors (M1 0.50, M2 0.05, M3 0.25, M4 0.10; M5 is handled by the
  report caveat).
- **Gates are applied before ranking:** `exclude` removes the candidate, and
  `review` keeps it but requires specialist sign-off.
- **The scores are documented judgements, not measurements.** Every number
  in the JSON has a stated reason, so it can be challenged.

## Stress tests

- **Robust across scenarios:** niacin and sirolimus are in the top 2 under
  every mechanism scenario. Niacin drops to second only under M4 (near-null),
  where there is little protein left to stabilise. That is the expected
  scenario dependence.
- **Scenario-sensitive:** arimoclomol ranks 3rd under M1 but 6th under M4. It
  is the candidate whose fate depends most on the Tier 1 experiments.
- **Leave-one-source-out** (`s4_stress.tsv`): **all three top candidates are
  flagged** for depending on a single source.
  - Niacin rests on North 2014 (*EMBO J*, 24825348); without it, niacin drops
    2 ranks.
  - Sirolimus rests on Sieben 2020 (*JCI*, 31738183); 3 ranks.
  - Dasatinib rests on Baker 2011 (*Nature*, 22048312); 4 ranks.

  This is the most important honesty point for the report. **The drug-level
  evidence base for MVA is thin.** Each lead rests on one key mouse paper,
  and none has E1 or E2 drug evidence.
- **Speculative-edge ablation:** halving c2 for candidates that rest on
  speculative edges (arimoclomol, phenylbutyrate, HCQ, metformin, JAK
  inhibitors) changes no ranks. The top three don't depend on speculative
  edges.
- **Prior uncertainty** (5,000 Dirichlet draws, concentration 10): the top 3
  are stable (P(top 3) ≥ 0.92). Arimoclomol enters the top 3 in 8% of draws,
  when the M1 weight is high.
- **Absolute scores are modest** (best 62/100 under M1). No candidate reaches
  the "direct BUB1B functional evidence" ceiling. That is consistent with
  these being hypotheses for follow-up, not recommendations.

## Shortlist for the report

1. **Niacin (Branch A, primary hypothesis).**
   - *Rationale:* NAD+ decline lowers BubR1 through SIRT2/K668 acetylation,
     and an NAD+ precursor (NMN) restores BubR1 in mice (North 2014).
     Niacin is the *approved* NAD+ precursor and raises human blood NAD+ up
     to 8× (Pirinen 2020). Higher BUBR1 is tumour-protective in mice (Baker
     2013, *NCB*), which addresses the cancer gate.
   - *Falsified if* niacin or NAD+ repletion fails to raise BUBR1-N1002K, or
     fails to reduce PCS, in patient cells.
   - *Counterarguments:* raised uric acid with nephrocalcinosis; NAD+ may
     support tumour metabolism; SIRT2 doesn't extend lifespan in WT mice
     (Wu 2023).
2. **Low-dose rapalog (Branch B/C).**
   - *Rationale:* mTORC1 hyperactivity tracks with sarcopenia in BubR1 MVA
     mice (Sieben 2020). Temsirolimus has been combined safely with RMS
     chemotherapy (ARST1431), though it gave no EFS benefit.
   - *Falsified if* the rapalog lowers BUBR1 in patient cells (the
     autophagy counterargument, 37094517), or doesn't reduce SASP or
     senescence.
3. **Dasatinib ± quercetin (Branch B).**
   - *Rationale:* clearing senescent cells delays progeroid phenotypes in
     BubR1 H/H mice (Baker 2011). Patent data show D+Q reduces senescence
     markers in this model.
   - *Falsified if* it enriches aneuploid survivors, or fails to clear
     senescent patient fibroblasts.
   - *Counterarguments:* healthspan without lifespan gain; senescence is a
     tumour barrier.

**Branch A mechanism-dependent alternate:** arimoclomol. Promote it if the
Tier 1 chase assay shows an HSP90-dependent unstable N1002K (M1 confirmed).

## Avoid list (from `candidates.json`)

- **HSP90 inhibitors:** they remove mutant BUBR1. This counts against
  17-AAG, even though it is "aneuploidy-selective" (Tang 2011).
- **Investigational MPS1, Aurora B and PLK1 inhibitors.**
- **p53 inhibition.**
- **Microtubule agents and radiotherapy:** these can't be avoided in RMS
  care. Flag them for oncology dose review.
